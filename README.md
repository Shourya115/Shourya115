## ◈ About Me

<img align="right" width="260" src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/tandpfun/skill-icons/main/icons/CSS-Dark.svg" alt="decorative"/>

I'm a frontend-leaning full-stack developer who likes taking a product from a blank Figma frame to a deployed, working app. Most of my time lives in **Next.js and React** on the frontend, **Firebase and Node.js** on the backend, and **Flutter** when the idea needs to be in someone's pocket instead of their browser.

I care a lot about the details most people skip — loading states, empty states, animation timing, the 200ms that makes an app feel "expensive." If a UI feels sluggish or clunky, I probably can't leave it alone until it's fixed.
